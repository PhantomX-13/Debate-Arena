Under the hood

How the product is designed

Switch connects people and role-based AI agents in a shared Discord room. The verified live setup used Codex for each role, with different role prompts. Prompts and rubrics are provider-independent; the current template still names Codex and this repo does not contain a universal model API adapter.

People in Discord→Switch bridge + room→Moderator→Scout→Advocates A + B→Coach→Fact-Checker→Judge→Human vote→Report

Before the debate, the Moderator invites each person to share a view or evidence about A, B, or neither. These views are attributed and checked as claims where possible. If the first ballot has no A/B majority, the group asks one focused question; the Scout researches it, the Fact-Checker checks the new claims, and the Judge revisits the ranking. The same options return for round 2.

Live status: Learning, Project, Research, Hackathon, and two MORE INFO follow-up cases have been exercised; both ended in A/B decisions. DA-7F07-L5 closed with B 1–0 after its original 4:27 PM close. The separate round-2 PENDING outcome is covered locally, but has not had its own live case.

Why separate agents?

Each role gets a focused task and can hand work to the next agent. This is a role-based workflow; it may use the same underlying model for multiple roles. People can follow the steps, contribute their own views, and vote in the shared room.

What stays uncertain?

Agents may share model blind spots. Separate roles do not guarantee better answers. A claim needs evidence, and people make the final decision.
